## Known failure modes

_Written from the failing cases above: what fails, how often per model, and whether a prompt change, a parser control or nothing is the right response._

**Run notes.** Three OpenAI models, the provider whose key the hosted instance
uses. The harness defaults to LiteLLM's bundled cost map, which predates
`gpt-6.1-terra`. This run loaded the current published map
(`LITELLM_LOCAL_MODEL_COST_MAP` empty), so all 180 calls were priced and the
budget applied. Ambiguous cases are scored separately.

1. **Equipment inferred where the note names none** (`gpt-5.4-mini` low 6 cases, medium 3; `gpt-5.4-nano` medium 1).
   - **Examples:** `en-back-squat-5x5`, `en-emom`, `en-long-session`, `pl-back-squat` and `pl-warmup-oov` get `barbell`; `mixed-rounds` and `en-cindy-amrap` get `bodyweight`.
   - **Why it fails:** the rule is that only an equipment word in the note sets equipment, and the smaller models fill it from the movement.
   - **Opposite error:** `gpt-6.1-terra` (`en-rounds-aliases`), `gpt-5.4-mini` medium and `gpt-5.4-nano` miss equipment that *is* written inside a movement name (`Barbell Row` in `en-long-session`, `Wall Ball` in `en-rounds-aliases`).
   - **Response:** a parser control rather than a prompt change. Keep a model's equipment only when an equipment word occurs in the note, and derive it from the matched alias when the name carries it.
2. **Out-of-vocabulary names mapped onto known movements** (every `gpt-5.4` configuration: OOV rejected 44%; `gpt-6.1-terra` 100%).
   - **Examples:** `pul ups` becomes Pull-up (`en-typo-oov`), `bieg` becomes Run (`pl-run-oov`), `skakanka` becomes Jump Rope (`pl-warmup-oov`), and the Polish names in `pl-polish-names` become Pull-up and Push-up.
   - **Why it matters:** the movement is often right, but the note never used a listed name or alias. Logging it silently would hide the gap that the "unmatched" list exists to show.
   - **Response:** a parser control that refuses an entry whose source text matches no listed name or alias and reports it as unmatched. The smaller models do not follow the prompt instruction alone.
3. **Missed listed aliases** (`gpt-5.4-nano`: low 14 failing cases, medium 11).
   - **Examples:** `KB Swing`, `OHS`, `Air Squat`, `Toes-to-bar`, `Handstand Push-up`, `Walking Lunge`. They are dropped or sent to unmatched although the vocabulary lists them (`en-kb-swings`, `mixed-ohs-bar`, `en-cindy-amrap`, `en-rounds-aliases`, `en-hspu-c2b`, `mixed-lunges`).
   - **Response:** none in the parser. `gpt-5.4-nano` is not a fit for this task.
4. **Single misses on the best model.**
   - `gpt-6.1-terra` medium leaves the duration empty in `en-row-time` (ambiguous, "7:45").
   - Low misses `ball` in `en-rounds-aliases`.
   - **Response:** nothing beyond item 1's equipment control.

**Choice.** `gpt-6.1-terra` at low effort is the server default this run supports.
- **Accuracy:** case exact 96%, entry exact 98%, OOV rejected 100%.
- **Cost:** about 0.11 USD per 100 notes, less than `gpt-5.4-mini` at either effort, which spends more on output.
- **Speed:** p50 2.9 s.
- **Medium effort** adds about 20% cost for one more exact case and one ambiguous miss, so it is not worth it.
